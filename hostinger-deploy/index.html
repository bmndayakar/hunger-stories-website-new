Our Food Philosophy • Our Story
Founded 2024 • Hyderabad, India

More Than
Just A Meal.

“Great food brings people together. Every recipe is crafted with deep respect for Indian culinary traditions, rich natural spices, and the joy of sharing a memorable meal.”

Founded in Hyderabad in 2024, Hunger Stories was born from a simple belief: everyday dining and grand celebrations alike deserve exceptional flavour, honest ingredients, and genuine hospitality.

From the aroma of our slow-dum biryanis unsealed fresh at your table to the smoky char of live tandoor grills and comforting curries, we cook food that creates lasting memories.
